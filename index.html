About Jazan

Jazan (also known as Jizan) is a vibrant port city and the administrative capital of the Jazan Province, nestled in the southwestern corner of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Positioned right along the stunning coast of the Red Sea, it serves as the primary gateway to the magnificent Farasan Islands. The city is celebrated for its rich cultural heritage, diverse geography ranging from pristine beaches to majestic mountain ranges, and its thriving agricultural sector, which produces high-quality tropical fruits like mangoes and figs. Today, Jazan is rapidly transforming into a significant economic powerhouse due to massive modern infrastructure projects, renewable energy developments, and the industrial growth driven by the Jazan Economic City initiatives.
